Strategic Planning

1.Choose on what you want to achieve academically.
Example: Improve your Maths grade from 50% to 70%.

2. Set clear and realistic goals for the term or year.
Example: Study 2 hours daily and complete all assignments on time.

3. Create a study schedule and stick to it.
Plan time for revision, homework, and group discussions.

4. Monitor your progress regularly.
Check your test results and adjust your study methods if needed.

Time Management

  • Create a realistic study schedule that balances school work, revision, and rest.
  • Prioritize important tasks and complete assignments before deadlines.
  • Minimize distractions such as social media while studying.
  • Track your academic progress and adjust your study plan when necessary.

Final Submission

  • Carefully proofread your work to correct grammar, spelling, and clarity errors.
  • Ensure proper formatting according to the required guidelines (font size, spacing, referencing style).
  • Submit assignments before the deadline to avoid penalties and demonstrate responsibility.
  • Keep a backup copy of your work to prevent loss of important files.
